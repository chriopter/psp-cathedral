## The windows

Every window in this program is drawn by its own code -- distance functions,
noise and a palette -- after a real one. No photograph, scan or drawing of any
window was copied, traced or used as pixels. The real works that the ten rooms
are modelled on are all long out of copyright; the ones still protected (the
Cologne window by Gerhard Richter, Chagall's glass, Matisse at Vence, the new
glass in Notre-Dame, Sagrada Família) are deliberately not among them.

| Room | After |
|---|---|
| 1 | Sainte-Chapelle, Paris — upper chapel, glazed 1248 |
| 2 | Chartres cathedral — north rose, c. 1235 |
| 3 | York Minster — the Five Sisters, c. 1250 |
| 4 | King's College Chapel, Cambridge — east window, c. 1515 |
| 5 | Le Mans cathedral — the Ascension window, c. 1120 |
| 6 | Santa Maria del Fiore, Florence — oculus after Donatello, 1434 |
| 7 | St Peter's, Rome — Bernini's Holy Spirit window, 1666 |
| 8 | St Vitus, Prague — art nouveau glass, 1931 |
| 9 | Franciscan church, Kraków — "Let there be!", 1904 |
| 10 | Honan Chapel, Cork — Harry Clarke's windows, 1916 |
